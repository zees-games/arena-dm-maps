# arena-dm-maps

Deathmatch and gun game maps for game-arena, a sibling of arena-surf-dm-maps. Read `../../CLAUDE.md`, `../game-arena/CLAUDE.md`, game-arena's `docs/imported-maps.md` and `../arena-surf-dm-maps/CLAUDE.md`: the layout, pack-per-map rule, `provides.json` per map and tag-to-release flow are the same.

Maps come from the 16 GameBanana mods in nightly item `map-suggestions-1`; the conversion is `maps-conv-arena-dm-1`. They go through the global importer in dot-map, never per-map patches. Every map needs an author and a source page in `maps.json` and the README table. Skip any map whose author forbids sharing or whose licence is outside the art rules.

**Map content is not in git** (`/maps/*` is ignored): it is delivered through the CDN publish flow, and `content.lock.json` (one `store` entry per map, `into: maps/<id>`) is how a clone gets it back: bootstrap fetches it before linking. Republish a map, then regenerate its lock line. What is committed is `maps.json`, the README credits, `zones/<id>.json` (the importer's attribution and `kind` per map, passed with `--zones-dir`) and `tools/`. Import: `python3 ../game-g2gfast/tools/bsp_import.py <bsp> maps --id <id> --zones-dir zones`.

`cp-orange-x3` (a Team Fortress 2 community map, Orange_X lineage) is published like the others under the credited-community-map exception (owner policy, 2026-11-07): Valve textures and models are replaced by the free ones in the converter rules, the credit carries a link and the removal-request contact. A map's `modes` list (zones file, copied into the manifest) says which modes it hosts; `flag_spawns` there is the capture-the-flag layout (adapt it to dot-spawn's flag kind when that lands).

**Publish rule: no source-decoded textures in a pack (owner policy, 2026-11-07).** `maps/<id>/textures/` (and any `sky_*.png`) is the importer's decode of the textures in the source map's pakfile: third-party or Valve art that is mapped to nothing free. `tools/stage_packs.sh` never copies it; a pack draws from `worlds/<id>/textures` (`fb_*` = the CC0 PBR fallback tree, `gen_*` = generated), the baked `.bin` and our own lightmap. `tools/check_packs.py` (run by `stage_packs.sh` on everything it stages, and unit-tested by `tools/test_check_packs.py` in `.github/workflows/check.yml`) fails on a root `textures/` folder, on any file named like an entry of the world's coverage `textures_unmapped` / `textures_flat` / `nearest_photo`, on .vtf/.vmt/.mdl/.bsp/.vpk or sound files, and on a hollow pack. Publish only what `stage_packs.sh` staged, never `maps/<id>` directly.
